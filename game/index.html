Flying the Isles

Keep above stall speed (watch the airspeed tape — below it the wing quits and the nose drops). Bank to turn; a steep bank needs back pressure or you sink. Line up on the runway early, gear down, throttle back, and touch down under 2 m/s sink for a perfect landing. Fuel burns faster at full throttle; an empty tank means a glide, not a flight. Rings glow gold — fly through the hoop, not past it. On the fire mission, skim the sea with the gear up, under 7 m and at 25–55 m/s, to scoop water; one tank holds three drops. Landings count only when the objectives are done.
